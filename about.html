ABOUT US

A small bakery with a big love for baking.

Sweet Crumbs Bakery is a fictional local bakery created for a web development project.

Our story

Sweet Crumbs started as a small home-based baking idea and grew through word-of-mouth recommendations. The bakery focuses on fresh, attractive and affordable treats for local customers.

Our goal is to make ordering a cake simple while giving customers friendly service and flexible choices.

Our mission

To create delicious baked goods and memorable experiences for every customer.

Our vision

To become a trusted community bakery known for quality, creativity and friendly service.

Our values

Quality

We care about presentation, freshness and consistent service.

Creativity

We enjoy turning customer ideas into unique cakes and treats.

Community

We aim to support and serve the local community through accessible products and service.
